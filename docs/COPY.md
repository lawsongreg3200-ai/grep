## HOME — What we check / what we don't (keep, plain terms)
We check: HTTPS and where a link really lands; whether Pricing, Privacy, and Terms
pages exist; claims on the page (pricing promises, metrics, testimonials, "as seen
in", guarantees); contradictions between the homepage and its pricing page; and
which third-party scripts the site runs.
We don't check: private data, anything behind a login, and we can't read intent.
If we can't verify something, we say Unknown — we never guess.

## METHODODOLOGY — plain-terms explainer sections
- What this product is (3 sentences, from Home promise + what we check).
- How a scan works: we fetch the page for real (plus its pricing/terms/privacy
  pages), extract claim sentences with rules (not AI guesses), and check each
  one against what those public pages actually say.
- How status is decided: Pass = a check we ran directly succeeded (e.g. valid
  HTTPS). Fail = we observed a direct contradiction (e.g. homepage says "free
  forever", pricing page requires payment). Everything else is Unknown, with the
  reason shown.
- Confidence: how directly the evidence source relates to the claim. High =
  found on the site's own linked page; Lower = inferred or partial.
- method_version + date on every report; method changes bump the version.
- Limitations: "This is not a legal verdict, not a scam guarantee, and it only
  checks public sources."
